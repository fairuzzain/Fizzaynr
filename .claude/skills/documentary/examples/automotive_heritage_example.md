# Case Study 2: Motorsport Heritage & Supercar Evolution
## 4-Beat Editorial Documentary Sequence (1968 Garage → Concept Hypercar)

This example demonstrates how to apply the 4-Beat Editorial Documentary formula to industrial design, automotive engineering, or motorsport history.

---

## 1. Sequence Overview

- **Subject**: Tracing the evolution of an endurance racing engine to a modern electric hypercar.
- **Duration**: 10.0 seconds
- **Format**: 16:9 Landscape

```
Beat 1 (00:00–00:04): 1968 pit garage wall + pop-in driver Polaroids & tachometer cutout.
Beat 2 (00:04–00:07): Whip-tilt to drafting table + 3D glowing isometric chassis wireframe extrusion.
Beat 3 (00:07–00:08): Match-cut snap zoom through the steering badge into a futuristic wind tunnel.
Beat 4 (00:08–00:10): 3D studio turntable hero orbit of the hypercar under dramatic studio strip lighting.
```

---

## 2. Step 1: Nano Banana Pro Base Stills

### Anchor Still 1: 1968 Pit Garage Wall
```text
Symmetrical wide photograph of an authentic 1968 endurance racing pit garage back wall. Dark metal pegboards on the left and right loaded with vintage wrenches, spark plugs, chrome velocity stacks, and oily rag stacks. In the open center weathered brick wall, bold distressed white stencil text reads 'LE MANS' above yellow block text '1968'. Moody cinematic documentary lighting, oil-stained concrete floor, warm tungsten work lamps, heavy atmospheric dust motes, 16:9.
```

### Anchor Still 2: Technical Drafting Table & Chassis Blueprint
```text
Top-down flat-lay photograph of an aerospace engineer's drafting desk. Centered dark slate-gray architectural grid mat with crisp white technical lettering reading 'CHASSIS MK-IV' in the middle. Flanked on left by brass drafting compasses and slide rules, on right by chrome engine pushrods and technical pens. Overhead daylight balanced drafting lamp, pristine technical documentary aesthetic, 16:9.
```

### Anchor Still 3: Modern Aerodynamic Wind Tunnel Facility
```text
Symmetrical wide shot looking down the barrel of an ultra-modern carbon-fiber aerodynamic wind tunnel testing chamber. Centered glowing circular intake turbine with the illuminated emblem 'MK-IV' in the hub. Bright white LED linear light strips recessed into the floor and ceiling. Futuristic automotive laboratory aesthetic, 16:9.
```

---

## 3. Step 2 & 3: Gemini Omni Flash Video Prompts

### Beat 1: 2.5D Cutout Pop-In & Depth-of-Field Blur (00:00–00:04)
- **Input**: Uploaded 1968 Pit Garage Wall still
- **Mode**: Image-to-Video

```text
Start from the uploaded 1968 garage wall still. Slow dramatic camera push-in. At 1.5 seconds, two vintage sepia Polaroid driver portraits labeled 'KENNETH DRAKE' (top left, tilted -7 degrees) and 'MARCUS BELL' (bottom right, tilted +5 degrees) pop into the foreground with a quick snappy spring bounce. Simultaneously, the background tool pegboards and brick wall smoothly rack-focus into a creamy f/2.4 blurred bokeh. The driver portraits are rigid matte paper cutouts. One continuous shot, no scene cuts. Gritty documentary archive style. Subtle engine idle rumble in background.
```

### Beat 2: Whip-Tilt & 3D Chassis Wireframe Extrusion (00:04–00:07)
- **Input**:
  - `<FIRST_FRAME>`: 1968 Pit Garage Wall
  - `<LAST_FRAME>`: Technical Drafting Table Still
- **Mode**: First + Last Frame Interpolation

```text
Transition smoothly from the first frame (garage brick wall) to the last frame (drafting table) with a rapid downward camera whip-tilt. The camera physically tilts down on its vertical axis with realistic vertical motion blur, locking top-down over the slate-gray 'CHASSIS MK-IV' mat. As the camera settles, a glowing white 3D isometric wireframe tubular spaceframe chassis extrudes upward from the grid lines, accompanied by floating cyan CAD dimension lines and callout labels: 'Twin Turbo V8', 'Monocoque Core', and 'Aero Venturi'. One continuous shot, oner, no scene cuts, physical camera whip-tilt.
```

### Beat 3: Match-Cut Turbine Zoom (00:07–00:08)
- **Input**:
  - `<FIRST_FRAME>`: Drafting Table with 3D Wireframe Chassis
  - `<LAST_FRAME>`: Modern Wind Tunnel Facility Still
- **Mode**: First + Last Frame Match-Cut

```text
One continuous camera move from the first frame to the last frame. The camera accelerates forward in an aggressive snap-zoom straight into the center emblem on the chassis nosecone, piercing directly through the metal mark and seamlessly bursting into the centered illuminated turbine hub of the modern wind tunnel chamber. One continuous shot, oner, no scene cuts, optical match-cut zoom. Wind tunnel suction air sound, heavy whoosh riser.
```

### Beat 4: 3D Studio Hypercar Hero Turntable (00:08–00:10+)
- **Mode**: Text-to-Video / Reference-to-Video

```text
Studio hero product shot of a sleek matte British-racing-green modern hypercar on a rotating polished black glass turntable. The camera executes a smooth low-angle 3D orbital tracking shot circling the front quarter. Dramatic overhead linear strip lighting glides across the sculpted carbon-fiber bodywork, with crisp amber aerodynamic flow-lines tracing along the front splitter. One continuous shot, oner. Low electric motor whine, high-frequency aerodynamic wind whisper, no music.
```
