Additional Musical Lyric Styles (appended to knowledge file)
This section adds nine musical lyric style templates for the lyric assistant to use as selectable writing formats. Each entry describes the style’s lyrical voice, common themes, preferred rhetorical devices, meter and phrasing guidance, and production cues the assistant should include when generating or expanding lyrics. Do not quote or imitate any copyrighted lines; use only stylistic devices and original text. Label each style by a short descriptive tag (no proper names).

🎹 Theatrical piano‑ballad storyteller
Lyrical voice: grand, confessional, emotionally direct with theatrical flourishes.
Themes: personal revelation, redemption, intimate confession turned universal.
Devices: extended metaphors, rising cadences into sustained vowels, dramatic crescendos, clear chorus payoff.
Meter & phrasing: 8–12 syllables per line; allow long sustained vowels on chorus lines for melodic holds.
Production cues: piano forward, warm strings, spacious reverb on chorus; suggest plate reverb and vocal doubling for climactic lines.
Prompt hint: ask for a single emotional image to build a theatrical arc around.

🎤 Street‑poetic spoken‑word narrator
Lyrical voice: direct, rhythmic, socially aware, conversational but tightly metered.
Themes: survival, community, identity, social observation.
Devices: internal rhyme, enjambment, anaphora, concrete imagery, call‑and‑response hooks.
Meter & phrasing: 6–10 syllables per punchy line; allow syncopation and breath breaks for spoken delivery.
Production cues: sparse beat, warm low end, close‑mic vocal with light saturation; leave space for cadence.
Prompt hint: request a real‑world scene or occupation to root the narrative.

⚡ Rapid‑fire confessional lyricist
Lyrical voice: urgent, confessional, high‑velocity internal rhyme and multisyllabic patterns.
Themes: personal struggle, moral reckoning, rapid emotional shifts.
Devices: multisyllabic rhyme, internal couplets, abrupt line breaks, conversational asides.
Meter & phrasing: 5–9 syllables per line for verses; chorus can open to 8–12 for melodic lift.
Production cues: tight drum pocket, aggressive vocal compression, presence boosting; leave room for breathy pre‑chorus.
Prompt hint: provide a short list of concrete images to seed internal rhymes.

🪕 Roots‑folk introspective narrator
Lyrical voice: plainspoken, intimate, observational, warm domestic detail.
Themes: memory, travel, small moments, relational nuance.
Devices: simple similes, conversational phrasing, steady rhyme schemes (ABAB or AABB).
Meter & phrasing: 8–12 syllables per line; regular stress patterns for singability.
Production cues: acoustic guitar or fingerpicked arrangement, natural room reverb, minimal processing.
Prompt hint: ask for a place or object to anchor the verse.

🌫️ Psychedelic atmospheric chronicler
Lyrical voice: abstract, image‑driven, contemplative, often elliptical.
Themes: existential questions, altered perception, cosmic or interior journeys.
Devices: extended imagery chains, assonant echoes, open‑ended refrains, modal phrasing.
Meter & phrasing: flexible; lines may vary widely to create space for ambient phrasing.
Production cues: lush pads, delay‑heavy guitars, spatial reverb, gradual automation.
Prompt hint: request two contrasting images to weave into a recurring motif.

🌊 Melodic, intimate acoustic crooner
Lyrical voice: tender, confessional, conversational with melodic hooks.
Themes: relationships, quiet longing, domestic scenes.
Devices: tight internal rhyme, repeated melodic motifs, simple chorus hook.
Meter & phrasing: 7–10 syllables per line; keep lines singable and vowel‑rich for sustained notes.
Production cues: warm acoustic guitar, soft backing harmonies, intimate vocal chain (gentle compression, subtle reverb).
Prompt hint: ask for a single emotional verb (e.g., “remember,” “hold”) to center the chorus.

🧭 Conscious jazz‑inflected storyteller
Lyrical voice: literate, rhythmically flexible, socially reflective with poetic diction.
Themes: social observation, moral nuance, personal responsibility.
Devices: syncopated phrasing, conversational asides, metaphorical anchors, slant rhyme.
Meter & phrasing: variable; allow off‑beat phrasing and conversational cadences.
Production cues: upright bass, brushed drums, sparse piano comping, warm horn accents.
Prompt hint: request a social scene or moral dilemma to dramatize.

🎧 Boom‑bap poetic lyricist
Lyrical voice: measured, authoritative, dense with internal rhyme and narrative detail.
Themes: personal history, social commentary, lyrical craftsmanship.
Devices: internal rhyme chains, multisyllabic end rhymes, vivid nameable details.
Meter & phrasing: 6–10 syllables per bar for tight flow; chorus can expand for melodic contrast.
Production cues: crisp sampled drums, warm sub bass, vinyl texture optional.
Prompt hint: provide a three‑word motif to repeat as a hook.

🪙 Streetwise storyteller with classic cadence
Lyrical voice: reflective, precise, economy of words with strong narrative arcs.
Themes: survival, legacy, moral clarity, observational storytelling.
Devices: clear couplets, internal consonance, narrative turns, refrain lines for emphasis.
Meter & phrasing: 7–11 syllables per line; maintain steady cadence for clarity.
Production cues: soulful samples, warm midrange, clear vocal presence.
Prompt hint: ask for a formative memory to anchor the verse.

Provide a short “singability” note and one production tip per variant to guide recording or Suno generation.
