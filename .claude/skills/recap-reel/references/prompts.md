# Prompts

## Clip prompt (Nexabot `sfv`, one per photo)
Keep the blocks; fill SHOT and ACTION from what is actually in the photo. One camera move per
clip and vary the moves across clips (push-in, arc/orbit, lateral track, pull-back reveal,
handheld follow, low-angle tilt-up, rack focus foreground→subject).

```
STYLE: real cinematic event recap footage, documentary behind-the-scenes look, full-frame cinema
camera, 35mm lens, shallow depth of field, warm natural venue lighting, soft highlight bloom,
subtle film grain, smooth stabilised gimbal motion. Keep every person, face, outfit, logo and the
whole scene exactly as in the start frame.
SHOT: <one camera move>, e.g. "slow gimbal push-in toward the two crew members from behind as they
walk forward into the crowd, gentle parallax on the booths".
ACTION: <natural motion that fits the photo>, e.g. "they keep walking and raise their cameras;
attendees in the background mingle, turn and pass by; screens glow".
AUDIO: soft ambient crowd murmur only, no music, no speech.
AVOID: no on-screen text, captions, numbers or new logos, no face changes, no morphing, no extra
people stepping in front of the camera, no cuts, no slow motion.
```

Product / food / venue photos (no people): SHOT = slow orbit or push-in, ACTION = light sweeps,
steam, reflections, background bokeh drifting.

## Music prompt (Nexabot `music`, 1 request)
Instrumental only, name a tempo, ask for an immediate start (no long intro) and hits for cuts:

```
Instrumental, no vocals. Energetic cinematic event highlight track, 125 BPM, punchy kick and
claps from the first second, driving bass pulse, bright synth stabs, short risers and whoosh
accents every 4 bars, confident modern corporate hype, clean mix, strong ending hit at about
30 seconds.
```
Variants: luxury launch (deep house, 120 BPM, warm piano chords), sports/youth (trap-hybrid
percussion, 140 BPM, 808), wedding/gala (cinematic strings + percussion, 110 BPM), tech expo
(future bass/electronic, 128 BPM). Avoid naming real artists or songs.
